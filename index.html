Frequently Answered Inquiries

What courier service is utilized for global shipments?
All shipments are dispatched via tamper-sealed armored express courier (DHL Express Worldwide or FedEx International Priority) with full insurance coverage, signature verification, and real-time biometric GPS tracking.
Are timepieces and audio devices covered under warranty?
Every Aura creation carries a comprehensive 3-year international warranty covering all mechanical movements, driver components, and craftsmanship. In addition, lifetime servicing and regulation are available at any of our flagship atelier locations.
How does the 30-Day Bespoke Guarantee work?
If any object does not completely fulfill your aesthetic or tactile expectations, notify our concierge within 30 days of receipt. We will dispatch a complimentary courier pickup from your residence and process a complete refund or exchange.
Can I request bespoke engravings or customized finishes?
Yes. We offer diamond-drag micro-engraving for titanium casebacks and hot-foil personalization for Tuscan leather goods. Simply contact our concierge desk prior to ordering.
